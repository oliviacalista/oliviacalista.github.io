(02) Achievement Campaign Strategy Financial Projection

B-Startion Business Case Competition 2025

Semifinalist · #Ngampusbarengby.U campaign

Aug 2025 BINUS University Team of three

Context

A national business case competition hosted by BINUS University, with participants from leading Indonesian universities. The case: help by.U, Telkomsel's digital-only provider, win over university students. A survey of Universitas Diponegoro students showed that 88% knew by.U, but only 10% were active users.

What I did

  • Designed an integrated marketing campaign concept, #Ngampusbarengby.U: a campus roadshow with brand ambassadors, a short-video challenge, offline tenants and free SIM cards.
  • Worked with the team on the business analysis (SWOT-TOWS, McKinsey 7S, STP, Porter's Five Forces) and the TAM–SAM–SOM market sizing, from 281.6 million people (IDR 11.26 trillion monthly) down to about 2.2 million Gen Z students in major cities (IDR 154 billion monthly).
  • Led the financial projection. Modeled revenue with a customer life journey: 75% trial, 15% retained and 10% loyal users at an average price of Rp30,000, for about 9,500 products sold per month.
  • Built the four-year feasibility projection covering revenue, cost lines and net cash flow, and tested it with NPV, IRR and payback period.
  • Visualized the concept with social media mockups and an activation booth concept.
